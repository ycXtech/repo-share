# Daily AI and hardware briefing → X posts

Use this mode for recent AI, hardware, and AI×hardware developments, including robotics, edge AI, devices, chips, tools, and relevant research. A supplied briefing is a lead list, not verified evidence. Read the original official sources before rewriting its claims. Reuse [voice-presets.md](voice-presets.md) for natural Chinese; do not imitate a named person or invent first-hand use.

## Find and verify

1. Use live search or official feeds/pages. Default to the last 24 hours in the user's timezone; widen to 48 hours only when needed and label the actual window. Respect a user-specified theme or date range. Prefer 3–5 substantive, distinct items to filler. Do not force equal category counts when the evidence is thin. Avoid repeating the previous briefing when its contents are available.
2. Open each original announcement, changelog, paper, product page, documentation, or official repository. Record its URL, publication date, event/release date when different, status (announced, released, research, preview, or available now), concrete capability, and material limits. Use third-party articles to find leads, not as the sole basis for final claims. Attribute vendor benchmarks and prices to the vendor and check region, plan, and availability. Do not present a rumor or an old announcement as today's release.
3. For each selected item, explain: what happened; why it matters to the requested audience; one practical way to try, learn, or watch it; and what is not yet available or independently verified. Distinguish an enduring learning point from a fresh news event. If no qualifying news exists, say so and offer a clearly dated learning brief instead.

## Deliver the briefing and posts

- Head the brief with its date, timezone, search window, and a one-sentence editorial takeaway. List each item with its actual date/status, specific facts, practical value or learning point, action, caveat, and clickable official link.
- Unless the user sets another count, write **three independent, copyable X short-post drafts** from the strongest items. Vary the angle across a new tool or product, an AI×hardware learning point, and a useful discussion question when the selected news supports them. Each draft starts with a specific hook, then gives the verified fact and a concrete observation or question. Include the official source URL in or immediately with the draft. Keep it brief, conversational, and informative; avoid press-release language, invented experience, and exaggerated claims. Check actual X composer length before claiming a post fits.
- Recommend one draft for that day with a short reason grounded in relevance and distinctiveness. Include one optional, doable 20-minute learning or testing task tied to a selected item. Adapt the subject and voice to the user's stated interests; do not assume their identity, expertise, or account persona.
- If the user asks only for a briefing or only for posts, deliver only that part. A daily request means generate it on demand; set up recurring delivery only when the user separately requests a schedule.

## Images from official sources

For each post that needs an image, inspect the item's official announcement, docs, product page, press kit, paper figures, or official demo. Select a visual that directly supports the draft: a real interface, device, measured chart, or documented feature. Capture the relevant official page or its official media with the available browser/computer tool, and save a usable local image when the environment allows. A user-provided image is also acceptable after checking its fit and provenance.

Open every saved image to check legibility, context, and claim alignment. Keep an image map with the post number, what the image shows, official page/media URL, capture date, and any credit or reuse note. Do not treat a code or article license as permission for every image. Do not remove attribution, crop away material qualifications, fabricate screenshots, or use generated pictures as evidence. If an official visual cannot be captured or reused, explain the gap and provide the source page; do not label an unverified external image URL as a ready attachment.
